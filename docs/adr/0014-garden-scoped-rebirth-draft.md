# Replace automatic rebirth income growth with a garden-scoped draft

Rebirth will award a choice of permanent improvement instead of adding an automatic coin bonus. Draft improvements form a separate collection focused on combat changes and synergies, while the existing coin-bought powerups retain ordinary numerical growth; economy draft choices must help preserve a sustainable progression path. This makes the rebirth reward a player decision rather than an automatic income increase.

Draft ownership and highest-cleared-level eligibility persist across rebirths but reset with New Garden, preserving its meaning as a fresh start. Achievement gates are deferred because player-wide achievements survive that boundary. Deeper runs should improve rebirth value without removing the path forward for stuck players.

Existing gardens retain earned coin bonuses and possessions, but receive no retroactive draft picks. This preserves earned strength without turning migration into a large sequence of reward choices. New rebirths grant drafts only. A repeatable income reward is available when no normal improvements are eligible, preserving a reason to rebirth after finite rewards are exhausted.

Rebirth commits before its offers are revealed, and those offers survive reopening the game. This makes the choice binding without allowing cancellation or reloads to generate a more favorable offer.

The confirmed design includes 12 improvements across Basic, Advanced, and Keystone strength bands. Weighted sampling favors stronger eligible offers after deeper runs without revoking previously earned eligibility. Prerequisites may reference either draft ranks or coin-bought powerup ranks, allowing the two collections to support one another without merging their acquisition routes.

The design is implemented in the shared Godot game. The [specification](../rebirth-draft.md) records the roster, selection odds, edge cases, initial tuning, and persistence behavior.
