# Carry personal progression across single player and multiplayer

Multiplayer is social play with independent fields, observation, and chat; players do not affect one another's combat or rewards. A player brings the same garden into either mode and keeps progress earned there, rather than starting a separate multiplayer garden or resetting for each shared game. This preserves the existing persistent progression experience, but means multiplayer must accommodate different levels and rebirth histories, and any future competitive mode would need to revisit progression eligibility and trust.
