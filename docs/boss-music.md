# Boss music

Design confirmed through the user interview and implemented in the shared Godot game.

## Confirmed requirements

- Provide five distinct boss music themes.
- Retain the playful chiptune character of the regular music, with faster, heavier, more urgent music for boss levels.
- Play boss music throughout an active boss level, starting on manual start or auto-start and ending when the level clears or ends in defeat.
- Boss music covers the regular enemies before the boss arrives and any remaining after it dies.
- Choose themes by boss level, independently of boss type: levels 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50 use themes 1 through 5; level 60 returns to theme 1, repeating thereafter. Retrying the same level uses the same theme.
- Crossfade between regular and boss music over half a second in either direction.
- Keep the existing boss entrance sound effect when the boss appears.
- Start the selected boss theme from the beginning on each attempt. When the attempt ends, resume regular music from where it left off.
- Loading a saved active boss level selects that level's theme and starts it from the beginning; exact music playback positions are not saved.
- Tactical pause leaves music playing. Losing window focus or turning Sound off pauses playback; playback resumes when the window has focus and Sound is on.
- Combat speed does not affect music speed.
- Losing focus keeps combat and Auto countdowns running while silencing music and gameplay effects. In multiplayer, View selects the other garden’s current theme and effects; the local garden keeps its running or paused state. See `rebirth-and-pause-menu.md`.

## Acceptance scenarios

- Starting level 10 manually or automatically crossfades to theme 1 before the boss spawns. Killing the boss while escorts remain does not change the music.
- Clearing a boss level or losing to any enemy returns to regular music, including during preparation and any next-level countdown.
- Level 50 uses theme 5 and level 60 uses theme 1, regardless of the four-type boss rotation. Rebirth repeats the same level-to-theme mapping.
- Retrying or restoring an active boss level starts its assigned theme from the beginning.
- Tactical pause preserves playback; focus loss and muting suspend it. Regaining focus while muted, or unmuting while unfocused, does not resume audible playback.
- Changing combat speed leaves music tempo unchanged.

## Audio assets

Five original 32-bar synthesized loops are generated by `python3 scripts/make_boss_music.py` directly into `game/assets/boss_music_1.wav` through `boss_music_5.wav`. They have distinct melodies, harmonies, bass figures, drum patterns, and tempos, with loudness matched to the garden score. No external samples are used.

| Theme | Name | Tempo | First level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Midnight March | 144 BPM | 10 |
| 2 | Bramble Chase | 156 BPM | 20 |
| 3 | Moonlit Mischief | 162 BPM | 30 |
| 4 | Thornwall Stomp | 150 BPM | 40 |
| 5 | Garden Guardians | 168 BPM | 50 |

The audio controller derives selection from the active level, independently of the four boss types. The scene synchronizes music on session changes, startup restore, import, and New Garden. Crossfades use real time and freeze while muted or unfocused. Regular music retains its playback position in memory; boss attempts restart their assigned loop.

## Verification

The dedicated `game/tests/music.gd` suite exercises 42 checks through the actual scene, including manual and automatic starts, clear and defeat, surviving escorts, repeat themes, import, New Garden, rebirth, loop boundaries, crossfade timing, playback position, mute/focus interactions, and speed independence. It uses the Dummy audio driver and never reads or writes the player's garden save. The suite is included in `scripts/test.sh`.

All five generated files pass peak, RMS loudness, and loop-boundary discontinuity checks. These checks do not constitute a subjective listening review.

## Background

- Boss levels occur every tenth level. Boss types cycle through caterpillar, cobra, spider, and hound.
- The regular background track is a 64-second original synthesized chiptune loop. The existing boss audio asset is a short entrance sound effect.
- Tactical pause leaves music playing. Focus loss and Sound off pause music playback; playback resumes only when focused and unmuted. Combat speed does not change music speed.
- Saved gardens retain gameplay state but do not retain a music playback position.
