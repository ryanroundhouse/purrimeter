# Ranger and Moth artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the [confirmed anti-air design](anti-air.md). Runtime assets are `game/assets/ranger.png` (1024 × 1536, six tiers and four poses each) and `game/assets/moth.png` (1254 × 1254, four flight poses in a two-by-two sheet). Both retain the generated PNG pixels and transparency unchanged. `scripts/measure_air_art.gd` measures alpha bounds into `game/ui/air_art.gd`; runtime rendering adds the Moth's hover and ground shadow and the Ranger's arrow trail.

The images were inspected in the desktop game, compact phone controls, native Android landscape controls, and the Ranger skin preview. Ranger's arrow shot reuses the existing synthesized Bolt sound.

## Ranger prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production transparent PNG sprite atlas for Purrimeter, a SNES-style 2D cat tower defense game. Crisp 16-bit pixel art with dark outlines, limited rich colors, square pixels, three-quarter top-down view facing right. A CALICO CAT ARCHER named Ranger: opaque cream-white fur, orange and charcoal patches, amber eyes, teal scarf and cape, a tall recurved bow aimed diagonally upward to shoot flying enemies. EXACTLY 4 columns by 6 rows on a 1024x1536 canvas, each cell 256x256. Every sprite fits within the central 200x200 of its cell with transparent gutters; no overlaps, clipping, labels, grids, shadows, detached effects or background. Each row a distinct upgrade tier; each column animation pose: idle, drawing bow, releasing arrow (keep arrow in bow), recovery. Consistent body size and anchor. Tier rows: 1 leather vest wooden bow; 2 teal hood reinforced bow; 3 bronze armor bronze bow; 4 silver armor teal cape silver bow; 5 gold armor small crown ornate gold bow; 6 white-gold armor emerald crown magnificent luminous teal bow. Cat ears and calico face readable in every sprite, genuinely transparent alpha everywhere outside character. No text.

## Moth prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production sprite atlas for Purrimeter, a SNES 16-bit pixel art garden tower defense game. One distinct flying enemy species: a small mischievous garden MOTH, fuzzy ivory body, feathery antennae, large amber eyes, paired broad lavender wings with dark plum outlines and gold eye spots. View three-quarter overhead, facing right. Crisp square pixel clusters, rich limited palette, strong readable silhouette. EXACT 2 columns x 2 rows on a square 1024x1024 PNG, four flight animation frames in reading order: wings raised, wings fully spread, wings lowered, wings half raised. Same body size, direction and anchor in each frame. Each moth entirely inside central 340x340 of its 512x512 cell, generous transparent gutters. No text, labels, borders, detached effects, ground shadow, floor or backdrop. Background must be genuinely fully TRANSPARENT alpha, including gaps between antennae and wings. Ivory body and wing details remain opaque. Only four isolated sprites.
