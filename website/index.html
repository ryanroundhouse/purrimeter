Plan the swarm’s route

A winding path gives your cats time to attack. Block the route completely and enemies tear through your patrol. Too much backtracking sends them into a frenzy. You can pause to build, upgrade, or repair.

Grow your patrol

Upgrade three cat roles through six tiers and discover permanent powerups as you take on tougher waves and bosses. Rebirth returns you to level one with your surviving cats, upgrades, and a bigger coin bonus.

Play alongside friends

Join up to two friends to watch their patrols and chat while defending your own garden. Each player controls their own pace and keeps the same progress when switching to solo play.

Installing Purrimeter

These builds are unsigned, and the Mac app isn’t notarized. Your computer may ask for an extra confirmation before opening the game.

Installing on macOS
  1. Unzip the download and open the macos folder.
  2. Drag Purrimeter.app to Applications, then open it.
  3. If macOS blocks this unnotarized app and you trust this download, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, find Purrimeter, and choose Open Anyway. On older macOS versions, look in System Preferences → Security & Privacy → General.

See Apple’s guide to opening downloaded apps for details.

Installing on Windows
  1. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
  2. Open the extracted folder and run Purrimeter.exe. Keep Purrimeter.pck beside it.
  3. If SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC” and you trust this download, choose More info → Run anyway, if offered.

Some Windows security settings or managed computers block unsigned apps entirely. See Microsoft’s explanation of SmartScreen.

Your garden saves automatically, so you can pick up where you left off.
